About me

Network engineer and SysAdmin with hands-on experience in Data Center environments, from fiber and copper cabling to servers, switches and incident handling. I also build Zabbix monitoring and provide remote IT support.

Fiber and copper cabling

  • Installing, troubleshooting and maintaining fiber optic and copper cabling in Data Centers
  • Single-Mode (SMF) and MultiMode (MMF) fibers and their applications
  • Ethernet categories Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6A, Cat7 and Cat8 and their use cases
  • Connectors: LC, SC, ST, FC and MPO/MTP
  • Fiber handling: minimum bend radius, cable routing and protection
  • Fiber cleaning with approved tools to prevent signal loss and contamination

Testing and troubleshooting

  • OTDR: fault detection, cable length, attenuation and splice loss
  • VFL (Visual Fault Locator): fiber breaks and continuity issues
  • Light Source Power Meter (LSPM): optical power and link performance
  • Fiber cleaning kits and cable testers
  • Common Data Center issues: fiber damage, failed ports, network outages and hardware failures

Data Center operations

  • Replacing failed hard drives in servers and validating RAID rebuild status
  • Server and network switch replacement with minimal downtime
  • Rack and stack, server migrations and equipment decommissioning
  • Installing, labeling and documenting network infrastructure to standards
  • Ticketing systems: incidents, change requests and service requests
  • Data Center procedures, access control policies and safety regulations

Equipment and security

  • Switches, routers, firewalls and patch panels
  • Enterprise equipment from Cisco, Juniper, HPE, Dell, Arista and Fortinet
  • Basic understanding of penetration testing concepts and security best practices
